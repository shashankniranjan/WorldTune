# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want WorldTune to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of background,
identity or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests
that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors
for repeated or serious violations.

Report problems to the maintainer through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/shashankniranjan/WorldTune/security/advisories/new)
form or by contacting [@shashankniranjan](https://github.com/shashankniranjan) directly.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
